### 🚀 What I'm Building

**🩺 [Medichive](https://medichive.in) — democratising healthcare**
> Co-founded healthcare-data venture working to make healthcare more accessible through data and ML. The home base for my independent ML and engineering work.

**🔍 [Reason-Over-Search](https://github.com/GaurisankarJ/reason_over_search)** — *RL-trained LLM agents for retrieval-augmented reasoning*
> Reproducing and extending **Search-R1** ([2503.09516](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.09516)) and **ReSearch** ([2503.19470](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.19470)) — language models that interleave search calls with chain-of-thought, trained end-to-end with **GRPO**. Reproduced the Search-R1 GRPO baseline on Qwen2.5-3B across 7 QA benchmarks, and now running reward-shaping ablations to probe how partial-credit signals affect tool-use behavior.
> `NeMo-RL` · `vLLM` · `SGLang` · `FAISS` · `Ray` · multi-GPU (H100/H200/B300)

**🐠 Aquascope — real-time fish tracking on the edge**
> **YOLOv8n + ByteTrack** running on a **Jetson Orin Nano** with a Logitech C920 — fine-tuned on aquarium data and exported to **TensorRT FP16**. Because why not bring edge ML to your fish tank.
> `Jetson` · `YOLOv8` · `ByteTrack` · `TensorRT`
